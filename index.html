Event Details

Here's what to expect each day. Most events take place at Madhavgarh Palace, Gurugram — we'll note anywhere that's different.

Day One

Welcome Party

Friday, February 20

Join us as we kick off the weekend with an evening of drinks, food, and good company — the perfect way to meet (or reunite with) everyone before the festivities begin. Location TBD — details to follow.

7:00 PM — Late

Evening Welcome Reception

Cocktails and a relaxed dinner. Location to be confirmed. Smart casual attire.

2
Day Two

Mehndi & Sangeet

Saturday, February 21

A day of colour and celebration at the palace — intricate henna in the afternoon, followed by an evening of dinner, music, dance performances, and a few surprises.

11:00 AM — 4:00 PM

Mehndi

Henna artists on-site for the bride and all guests. Bright, festive attire.

7:30 PM — Late

Sangeet

Dinner followed by an evening of dance, music, and performances from both families — plus a dance floor open to all.

3
Day Three

Haldi & Wedding

Sunday, February 22

The big day. We begin with the turmeric blessings of Haldi, move into our traditional wedding ceremonies, and close the celebration with an evening reception.

9:00 AM — 11:00 AM

Haldi

A joyful turmeric ceremony blessing the couple. Wear something you don't mind getting a little yellow.

12:30 PM — 4:00 PM

Traditional Wedding Ceremonies

Baraat, Jaimala, and the sacred rites around the mandap, led by our pandit.

7:00 PM — Late

Evening Celebration

Drinks, dinner, and dancing to close out the weekend in style.
